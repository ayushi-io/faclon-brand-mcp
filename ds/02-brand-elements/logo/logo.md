# Logo usage

Source: Figma "Design MCP", Logo page.

**Blue logo.** The original blue logo is used on white and light-themed backgrounds, for strong visibility and clear brand recognition.

**White logo.** The white logo is used on dark-themed backgrounds and on Faclon brand-coloured backgrounds, where it gives strong contrast and keeps the logo clearly visible.

**Logos in the file:** Faclon Labs blue, Faclon Labs white, Bruce AI for dark backgrounds, Bruce AI for light backgrounds.

Always place the real logo file, from the asset library: `get_brand_guidelines(section: "assets", asset_category: "logos")` (Faclon Labs, Bruce AI, DeepSense; client and partner logos under "client-logos" and "partner-logos"). Never typeset the company name in its place, and never redraw or recolour the logo.

**Logomark.** The Faclon Labs logomark is the mark on its own, without the wordmark (blue and white versions in the Figma file). By default, always place the full logo. Use the logomark only in the specific places where the user explicitly asks for it; never swap it in for the full logo on your own. The same blue-on-light and white-on-dark rules apply.

Not yet defined in Figma: clear space, minimum size, and misuse examples.

**Co-branding.** When Faclon Labs appears with a partner, use one horizontal lockup: the Faclon Labs logo first, a vertical divider, then the partner logo, centred on one line. With H the height of the Faclon Labs logo, leave about three quarters of H on each side of the divider; the divider is a 1px line (border-width/default) in border/strong, about three quarters of H long; the partner logo is about three quarters of H tall and never taller than the Faclon Labs logo. Both logos are the real files, unchanged. On dark or brand backgrounds use the white Faclon Labs logo and the partner's version for dark backgrounds; if there is none, ask. The reference is the Faclon Labs and HPE lockup in the asset library (`partner-logos`).

**Product marks.** I/O Connect, Deepsense, Forge, Foundry, I/O Sense, UNS Dark and UNS Light, in the asset library (`logos`).

**Position.** By default the logo sits in the top-right corner, in every format. A design may place it elsewhere when the layout needs it, for example a centred newsletter header, or a slide whose top right is taken by a tab row or image.
