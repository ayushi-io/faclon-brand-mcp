# Skills index

One skill per outcome. Pick the outcome from the request; its folder holds `rules.json` (format id, defaults, who decides, what is fixed) and `SKILL.md`. Its canvas is in `01-foundations/grid-layout/<format>.json` and its taste (must not, avoid, design direction) in `06-taste/per-format-rules/<format>.json`. Through the MCP: get_brand_guidelines(section: "rules", format_profile: <format>).
