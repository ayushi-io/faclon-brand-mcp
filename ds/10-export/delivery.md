# Delivery

Finished HTML is delivered in the chat only: give the user the file, named as in `naming.md`, in the conversation. Do not upload it anywhere else, send it, or publish it.

With the file, add a short note of anything flagged for the user: content that did not fit, a suggestion waiting for approval, images or logos still to supply, and for newsletters the list of placeholders with their display and upload sizes.

The user converts the HTML to PDF, PNG, JPG or PPTX themselves when they need to; every outcome exports HTML only.
