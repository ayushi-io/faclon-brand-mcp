# Good vs bad examples

Real work the brand team reviewed, with what they said, what went wrong and the rule that now prevents it. Read these before designing in the same format: each one is a mistake that has already been made once.

Images are served at `%EXAMPLES_URL%/<file>`; "good" references point to the template thumbnails and reference slides to follow.

## Deck: Cyclone Jam prediction (reviewed 2026-10-28)

A 17-slide technical proposal generated by Claude from the deck skill, reviewed by the brand team in Figma. The brand team's comments are quoted as written. Every rule named below is in the deck profile (`get_brand_guidelines(section: "rules", format_profile: "deck")`).

The pattern behind most comments: content was poured into rows of same-size text cards, cards were stretched to fill the slide, and the slide's own shape (a sequence, a loop, a problem against a solution, a system) was never drawn.

### Cover

![Bad: cover](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/cover.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "looks empty need to have proper content/type size distribution"
- **What went wrong:** the title sits in the upper half and a small details box is pinned to the bottom edge, leaving a wide empty band between them; nothing on the left balances the dot form.
- **Do instead:** the title, subline and details form one group, placed together; raise the type a role before leaving space empty. Rules: `density`, decision `deck-density`.
- **Good:** the cover template ![Cover template](%TEMPLATES_URL%/decks/thumbnails/cover-headline-stats-and-contact.png)

### Team

![Bad: team](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/team.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "padding is missing, this can be designed in a infographic"
- **What went wrong:** five people are bare text rows running to the safe-area edge with no container or padding; the lead's card is tall with a gap in the middle.
- **Do instead:** draw the team as people (content form `people`): a grid of person cards with role tags and credentials as stats, each block with padding; headshots from the asset library where they exist, and ask the user when they do not. Rules: `density`, `text-card-grids`.
- **Good:** ![Team template](%TEMPLATES_URL%/decks/thumbnails/team-grid-with-stats-and-map.png)

### Problem statement and project objectives

![Bad: problem statement](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/problem-statement.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "too much empty space"; "if this is a propblem statemnet, why its not in red?"
- **What went wrong:** the six objective cards are stretched to equal heights around two or three lines each. The problem side is neutral grey, with red only on its chip and one heading.
- **Do instead:** a problem and solution split (content form `problem_solution`): the problem side in the Negative intent (red container tint, red headings or icons), the objectives in brand; cards sized to their content. Rules: `colour-by-meaning`, `stretched-cards`, decision `problem-negative`.

### System architecture

![Bad: system architecture](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/system-architecture.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "where is the architecture design langiage"
- **What went wrong:** the architecture is generic cards joined by thin arrows; none of the architecture format's zones, device styles, icons or colour rules are used.
- **Do instead:** draw it in the architecture format's language: zones, the four device styles with icons from the architecture icon library, its arrows and colours, and the I/O Sense outcome when the diagram ends in I/O Sense, at 14px or more. Rules: `architecture-slides`, decision `architecture-in-decks`.
- **Good:** ![Architecture template](%TEMPLATES_URL%/architectures/thumbnails/cloud-architecture-substations.png)

### Ticketing workflow

![Bad: ticketing workflow](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/ticketing-workflow.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "needs to be designed in infographic"
- **What went wrong:** seven steps are small numbered dots on a hairline with paragraphs hanging under them; the top third of the slide is empty and three unrelated cards fill the bottom.
- **Do instead:** a flow (content form `sequence`): numbered nodes with an icon each, connectors, short text under each node, sized to fill the content area. Rules: `infographic`, `icons`, `density`.
- **Good:** ![Numbered steps template](%TEMPLATES_URL%/decks/thumbnails/three-numbered-steps-with-metrics.png)

### Solution approach

![Bad: solution approach](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/solution-approach.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "why such long UI cards, with so much empty space"
- **What went wrong:** four cards are stretched to the bottom of the slide with their text in the top third; the model flow, the real story of the slide, is a row of small chips.
- **Do instead:** make the model flow the hero diagram and size the cards to their content. Rules: `stretched-cards`, `density`, `text-card-grids`.

### MLOps lifecycle

![Bad: MLOps lifecycle](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/mlops-lifecycle.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "needs to look as lifecycle not all ui cards"; "contrast is missing"; "aligment is wrong"; "can have icons"; "these both can be designed as infographic"
- **What went wrong:** a lifecycle drawn as six cards in a row; the highlighted step is filled with a dark blue (brand/900) that disappears into the dark background; the "Retraining" label wraps and does not line up with its text; the tooling list has no icons; tooling and security are two plain text panels.
- **Do instead:** a loop diagram (content form `lifecycle`) with arrows back to the start; highlight with container/brand; labels on one line and one baseline with their text; one Lucide icon per tool; tooling and security as icon grids. Rules: `infographic`, `dark-highlight`, `alignment`, `icons`, `text-card-grids`.

### Cyclone blockage risk prediction on I/O Sense

![Bad: product risk view](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/product-risk-view.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "add dashoard mockup and make it light theme"
- **What went wrong:** a product slide set dark, with the screen described through coloured cards, tabs and bands instead of shown.
- **Do instead:** light theme with a dashboard mockup beside the user's points; until product screenshots are in the asset library, a `DASHBOARD_MOCKUP_<NAME>` placeholder frame, listed in the delivery note. Never draw a fake product UI. Rules: `product-slides`, `fake-product-ui`, decision `product-slides-light-with-mockup`.

### Root cause to recommendation

![Bad: root cause](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/root-cause.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "needs to designed as a infographic"
- **What went wrong:** the four steps sit on a connector line that runs through their titles, and the evidence and actions are three text panels.
- **Do instead:** a flow (content form `sequence`) from the example alert through evidence to actions, with the alert as the hero visual; connectors never cross text. Rules: `infographic`, `alignment`.

### Closing

![Bad: closing](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/closing.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "this slide is completely wrong, check design again"
- **What went wrong:** the closing reference slide was redrawn from a description: the light streak became a large glowing visual on the right and the dot form was missing.
- **Do instead:** place the closing reference slide's ready HTML (`reference_slides.slides.closing.html`) as it is and fill only its tag and statement. Rules: `use-reference-slides`, decision `dot-particle-on-brand`.
- **Good:** the same statement in the closing reference ![Good: closing](%EXAMPLES_URL%/deck-2026-10-28/closing-reference.jpg)

## Social: cement plant energy efficiency carousel (reviewed 2026-10-30)

A five-slide LinkedIn carousel (1:1) generated by Claude from the social media skill. Every rule named below is in the social profile (`get_brand_guidelines(section: "rules", format_profile: "social")`).

![Bad: cement plant carousel](%EXAMPLES_URL%/social-2026-10-30/cement-carousel.jpg)

- **Brand team:** "it doesnt look like its from ffaclon. the content is very dense, there are no infographics, no design element no ui cards. it has also not taken the logo"
- **What went wrong:**
  - Every slide has a "Faclon Labs logo" placeholder box instead of the logo file from the asset library.
  - The content slides are all light, while the brand team's templates use brand blue for step posts and dark for stat tiles; the set reads as plain white slides.
  - The problem is three narrow text cards pinned to the bottom with the top half empty; "What changed" is a bulleted list; the results are table rows of small numbers.
  - No design element on any slide.
- **Do instead:**
  - Place the logo file (`logos/faclon-labs-logo-white` on dark and brand, `-blue` on light) by its url. Rule: `logo`, decision `logo-from-asset-library`.
  - Start each slide from the social template whose layout fits, and pick its theme from it. Rules: `start-from-templates`, `themes`, decision `social-themes-from-templates`.
  - Draw each slide in its content form: the problem as a split in the Negative intent (content form `problem_solution`), what changed as an icon flow or icon tiles (`like_items`), the results as a big number with a visual or stat tiles (`numbers`). Rules: `text-card-grids`, `infographic`.
  - Put text and stats in UI cards with padding/container, sized to their content. Rules: `cards`, `spacing-by-purpose`, `stretched-cards`, `density`.
  - Use the design elements on any slide whose background allows them. Rule: `design-elements`, decision `design-elements-anywhere`.
- **Good:** the brand team's step and results posts ![Step post with a big stat and icon flow](%TEMPLATES_URL%/social-media/thumbnails/step-big-stat-with-icon-flow.png) ![Results stat tiles](%TEMPLATES_URL%/social-media/thumbnails/results-stat-tiles.png)
