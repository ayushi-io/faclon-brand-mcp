# Templates: decks

21 deck templates in seven sections (cover page, page breaker, content slides, product stack, dashboard and product slides, team slide, closing slide). The Figma reference slides on the Layouts page stay in `reference-slides.json`. Each entry in `templates.json` has its section and purpose, theme, layout notes, Figma node and a thumbnail in `thumbnails/`. Served by `get_brand_guidelines(section: "templates", format_profile: "deck")`, thumbnails at `/templates/decks/thumbnails/<file>`.

Templates show layout, composition, hierarchy and rhythm only. Never copy their text, numbers, claims, client names or logos, and build every value from the design system.
