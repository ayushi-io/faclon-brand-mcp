# Typography

Source: Figma "Design MCP", typography page and text styles.

- **TASA Orbiter** is the default heading font across website and marketing assets.
- **Inter** is the default body font across website and marketing assets.
- **Two typefaces only.** No third typeface anywhere, not even as a fallback. If TASA Orbiter is unavailable, use Inter and stop there.
- Weights from Regular (400) to Bold (700); Thin, ExtraLight, Light, ExtraBold and Black are banned. Sizes are whole pixels, never below 10px.
- Text styles: Display XL/LG/MD, Heading H1–H6 (each with Bold, Semi Bold and Medium variants), Eyebrow, and two Body ranges in Inter:
  - **Large body:** Body/72, 60, 48, 40, 32, 24, on the same size steps as the headings, for statements, stats and pull quotes. -1% letter spacing.
  - **Body copy:** Body/XL 20, LG 18, MD 16, SM 14, XS 12, 2XS 10. Regular, no letter spacing.
- Every Display, Heading and Body style comes in Regular, Medium, Semi Bold and Bold (for example Heading/H2/Bold, Body/MD/Medium). The base style's weight is the default.
- Eyebrow: Inter Medium, 10px / 16px, uppercase, positive letter spacing, colour text/eyebrow (brand/500).
- Sizes switch between Desktop and Mobile; for example Display XL is 72px on desktop and 40px on mobile.
